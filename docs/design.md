# Design

The direction every page follows.

## Identity

- A quiet research workspace for checking dalil, for listeners, students of knowledge and teachers. Calm, exact, trustworthy.
- Scripture is the hero. Every screen puts a verse or hadith, its source and its grades in front of the reader.
- Motif: scripture set in a naskh face, right to left, larger than everything around it, with the source line directly under it.

## Layout

- A left sidebar on a slightly lighter surface: the wordmark, the destinations, recent questions and the sample lectures. It collapses on desktop and becomes a drawer on mobile.
- The main area is an inset panel with rounded corners on a near-black page.
- Home: one question, one large input, suggestion chips under it.
- On a report, Ask docks to the right of the text.
- Language and theme switches sit top right.

## Colour

- Neutrals for the chrome: near-black page, charcoal sidebar and inputs, white and grey text. The light theme mirrors it on warm off-white.
- Status colours carry meaning and nothing else: green for verbatim and paraphrase, amber for misquote and weak or disputed, grey for not found. Each status also has its own shape, so colour is never the only signal.
- No gradients, no glow.

## Type

- Interface: IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Sans Arabic, one family for both scripts.
- Quran: KFGQPC Uthmanic Hafs (self-hosted). Hadith: Scheherazade New and Amiri.

## Shape and motion

- Radius: large on the panel and the main input, medium on cards, small on chips and buttons.
- Motion: hover and focus states only. No entrance animations.
